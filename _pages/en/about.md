I am a certified secondary-school teacher of History and Geography and a researcher in contemporary history. My research focuses primarily on social and economic history, with particular attention to gender, material culture, objects, and everyday practices.

My approach combines economic history, gender history, and the history of material culture in order to explore how norms, representations, and practices are shaped through everyday life. I am particularly interested in the social uses of objects, cultural circulation, and forms of individual and collective appropriation.

Alongside my research activities, I teach History, Geography, and Civic and Moral Education at lower-secondary level in the Grenoble education district. This dual position as teacher and researcher informs my interest in the transmission of historical and geographical knowledge, inquiry-based learning, and the different ways in which academic research can be brought into dialogue with secondary education.

My research is primarily situated within the field of contemporary history and revolves around several complementary areas of interest.

### Gender History

I am interested in the ways gender norms are produced, disseminated, negotiated, and sometimes challenged through everyday practices.

### Material Culture and the History of Objects

Objects provide a particularly productive lens through which to examine social change. Their production, circulation, uses, and associated meanings make it possible to explore social relations, identities, and cultural practices.

### Social History of Everyday Practices

My research pays particular attention to everyday gestures, uses, behaviours, and forms of appropriation, which provide a concrete means of understanding broader transformations in contemporary societies.

### Circulation, Mobility, and Cultural Transfers

I am also interested in the circulation of people, objects, models, and practices, as well as in the transformations produced when they move from one social or geographical context to another.
